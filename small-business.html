FAQ

Questions, answered.

We already have antivirus.

Antivirus checks files once they're on the laptop. Zaksha stops the laptop reaching scam and malware sites in the first place.

Does Zaksha monitor staff?

No. It only sees which sites a device looks up. It never reads messages, takes screenshots or records keystrokes.

Who looks after it if I'm away?

Rules keep running, and you can add a colleague as an Admin or a Viewer.

What about Android phones?

Android phones use Android's built-in Private DNS. If a phone stops reporting, we flag it as Gone quiet on your Activity page.

Who can see our records?

You choose who in your organisation can see activity. See how we handle your data.

Does it work for staff working from home?

Yes. Rules follow each device on any Wi-Fi or mobile data.

How long are records kept?

30 days by default. Your contract can set longer.
