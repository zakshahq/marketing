How it works

It stays on, without anyone looking after it

Zaksha runs on each device and checks every website name before the device can reach it. Blocked sites don't load, in the browser or in the app.

  • On Windows, staff without admin rights can't stop or remove it.
  • Switching to an encrypted DNS service or iCloud Private Relay doesn't get around it.
  • A device that goes quiet for two days while the others are still active is flagged in the console.
  • On supervised iPhones, iPads and Macs the profile can't be removed.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

We already have antivirus.

Antivirus checks files once they're on the laptop. Zaksha stops the laptop reaching scam and malware sites in the first place.

Will staff feel watched?

Zaksha sees which sites a device looks up, not what's on them. It doesn't read messages, take screenshots or record keystrokes, and you get a notice to tell staff it's on.

Who looks after it if I'm away?

Nobody needs to. Rules keep running, and you can add a colleague as an Admin or a Viewer.

What about Android phones?

They use Private DNS. It works, but someone with access to Settings can turn it off; the console flags a phone that goes quiet.

Who can see our records?

Only the people you add. Zaksha support can sign in to help, and every support sign-in is recorded. See how we handle your data.

How long are records kept?

30 days by default. Your contract can set longer, up to a year.
