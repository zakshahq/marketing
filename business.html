Questions

Common questions.

Which devices can Zaksha filter?

Devices your institution owns. Windows PCs and laptops run the Zaksha agent, and iPhones, iPads and Macs use configuration profiles. Personal devices are not enrolled.

What can an admin see?

Per-device activity: the domain names a device asked for and whether each was blocked or allowed. Zaksha never sees page content or messages. The console also alerts you when a device has gone quiet.

Can someone switch it off?

On Windows the agent runs as a system service, and a standard user account can't stop it. Anyone with the admin password, or who wipes and reinstalls Windows, can still remove it. Filtering done at the network level can also be bypassed with a VPN or a browser's own encrypted DNS.

Where is our data kept?

In India, on AWS Mumbai. A dedicated, isolated instance is available on paid contracts.

How does billing work?

You pay per device, with GST invoicing. The price is ₹79 per device per month, or ₹65 billed yearly, with a minimum of 10 devices. A 3-month pilot costs 10% of the quote.
