Why not a firewall or a monitoring tool?

No box to buy

A firewall only covers devices on your network. Zaksha runs on the device, so the rules apply on campus Wi-Fi, at home and on mobile data.

Not surveillance

Zaksha sees which sites a device looks up. It does not read messages, record keystrokes or take screenshots.

Your data stays in India

Filtering and activity records are stored in India (AWS Mumbai) and handled under the DPDP Act, 2023. A dedicated, isolated instance is available on paid contracts.

Indian pricing and support

Prices in rupees, GST invoices, and support on WhatsApp from people in India.

Which devices

  • Windows PCs and laptops — the Zaksha agent; staff and students without admin rights can't remove it.
  • iPhones, iPads and Macs — a configuration profile; can't be removed on supervised devices.
  • Android phones and tablets — Private DNS; works, but someone with access to Settings can turn it off. We'll tell you which setups to avoid.
  • Students' own phones on campus Wi-Fi — possible with a dedicated instance your network points at. A VPN gets around network filtering, and we say so up front. Ask us.

Common questions

Does Zaksha read messages or record screens?

No. It sees which sites a device looks up and whether they were blocked. Nothing else.

Can someone switch it off?

On Windows, not without admin rights. On supervised Apple devices, no. On Android, someone with access to Settings can, so we'll help you set it up to make that harder and tell you when a device stops reporting.

We already have a firewall. Do we need this?

A firewall filters your network. Zaksha filters the device, so it also covers laptops and tablets that leave the building, and it gives you per-device records a firewall usually doesn't.

Where is our data kept?

In India, on AWS Mumbai. A dedicated instance is available on paid contracts.

How does billing work?

A quote per device, monthly or yearly, with a GST invoice. The pilot is billed at 10% of that quote.
