FAQ

Questions, answered.

Why pay when Family Link is free?

Family Link is a solid free option if your child only uses an Android phone tied to a supervised Google account. Zaksha is for households with a mix of devices — Android, iPhone, Mac, Windows — that want one dashboard, filtering that's built to stay on, and a WhatsApp alert the moment filtering stops on any of them.

Can my child just turn it off?

We'd rather be straight about this: filtering is a strong layer, not a magic wall. On a device where a determined teen can change settings or install a VPN, DNS filtering is bypassable — protect settings with a Screen Time or device PIN. On Android, the tamper-proof setup locks Zaksha on so it can't be uninstalled from the phone, and we alert you on WhatsApp the moment filtering stops on any device.

Do I need a Google account for each child?

No. Zaksha sets up on the device itself, so there's no supervised Google account to create or migrate later.

What exactly can Zaksha see?

Website names only — that a device asked for youtube.com, never your messages, photos, passwords or anything on a page. History is kept for a rolling 7 days, then genuinely deleted. Everything is processed and stored on our servers in India and never leaves them. No ads, and nothing is sold.

How much does it cost?

The Home plan is ₹199/month, or ₹1,788 billed annually (₹149/month), for up to 5 devices across the household. Pay by UPI or card, cancel anytime.
