# Zaksha brand icons

Rendered from the canonical shield mark (marketing favicon.svg path, teal #0e8088).
Three variants, each at 1024 / 512 / 192 / 120 / 64 px:

- `zaksha-appicon-white-on-teal-*.png` — white shield on solid teal. Use for app icons
  where the platform shows a square/rounded tile: Meta app icon (1024), Play/App Store
  style listings, social profile pictures.
- `zaksha-appicon-teal-on-white-*.png` — teal shield on white. Use where the platform
  dislikes transparency or sits on white UI: Google OAuth consent-screen logo (120 min,
  upload the 512), Google Ads business logo.
- `zaksha-mark-teal-transparent-*.png` — the mark alone, transparent background. Use in
  documents, overlays on light backgrounds, ad creative.

Web favicons already live in the marketing repo (favicon.svg, icon-192/512, apple-touch-icon).
