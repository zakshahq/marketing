FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which devices does Zaksha cover?

iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows, all from one dashboard — so the same rules apply across every device your child uses, not just the Apple ones.

Can my child just turn it off?

Straight answer: filtering is a strong layer, not a magic wall. On iPhone, switching Zaksha's filtering off pauses the phone's apps until it's back on and alerts you on WhatsApp — so it's hard to switch off without you knowing. A determined teen who has the device passcode can still change settings; a Screen Time passcode on the phone holds them — though Apple lets the Apple ID password reset that passcode, so your Apple ID password is the real key.

What exactly can Zaksha see?

Website names only — that a device asked for youtube.com, never your messages, photos, passwords or anything on a page. History is kept for a rolling 7 days, then genuinely deleted. Everything is processed and stored on our servers in India and never leaves them. No ads, and nothing is sold.
