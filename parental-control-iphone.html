FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which devices does Zaksha cover?

iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows and Mac, all from one dashboard — so the same rules apply across every device your child uses, not just the phone.

Can my child turn it off? Is Zaksha tamper-proof?

Straight answer: filtering is a strong layer, not a magic wall. On a family iPhone, set a Screen Time passcode only you know and Zaksha can't be switched off or deleted — turning off filtering just locks your child's apps until it's back on. The one thing outside our control there is your Apple ID password, because Apple lets it reset the Screen Time passcode — so keep it private. If it's ever switched off, you get a WhatsApp alert within minutes.

Does it use a VPN or slow the phone down?

Zaksha filters at the network (DNS) level and runs quietly in the background — it doesn't read your traffic or route it through a slow server, and there's nothing to keep open. Battery and speed impact is negligible.

How do I set it up?

Install the Zaksha app from the App Store, follow the guided steps to connect it to your account, and set a Screen Time passcode only you know so it stays put. It takes a few minutes, then runs on its own.

What exactly can Zaksha see?

Website names only — that a device asked for youtube.com, never your messages, photos, passwords or anything on a page. History is kept for a rolling 7 days, then genuinely deleted. Everything is processed and stored on our servers in India and never leaves them. No ads, and nothing is sold.
