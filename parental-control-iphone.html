FAQ

Questions, answered.

Isn't Screen Time enough?

Screen Time is a good free start on a single iPhone. Zaksha is for families with more than one device that want adult-content filtering that works the same everywhere, apps that pause if filtering's switched off, and a WhatsApp alert the moment it stops.

Can my child just turn it off?

Straight answer: filtering is a strong layer, not a magic wall. On iPhone, switching Zaksha's filtering off pauses the phone's apps until it's back on and alerts you on WhatsApp — so it's hard to switch off without you knowing. A determined teen who has the device passcode can still change settings; set a Screen Time passcode to hold them.

Does it work on more than iPhone?

Yes. iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows all run from the same dashboard, so the rules follow your child across every device — not just the Apple ones.

What exactly can Zaksha see?

Website names only — that a device asked for youtube.com, never your messages, photos, passwords or anything on a page. History is kept for a rolling 7 days, then genuinely deleted. Everything is processed and stored on our servers in India and never leaves them. No ads, and nothing is sold.
