FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which devices does Zaksha cover?

Windows, iPhone, iPad, Android and Mac, all from one dashboard — so the same rules apply across every device your child uses, not just the PC.

Can my child just uninstall it?

Straight answer: filtering is a strong layer, not a magic wall. On Windows, Zaksha installs machine-wide and runs as a system service that a standard (non-administrator) account can't stop or uninstall — so when your child uses a standard account and you keep the admin password, they can't remove it, and if it ever stops you get a WhatsApp alert. Someone with the administrator password — or who wipes and reinstalls Windows — can still remove it: the admin password is the real key, the same way the Apple ID password is on iPhone. If your child's own account is an administrator, treat Zaksha as tamper-evident on that PC — you're alerted if filtering stops, rather than relying on them being unable to remove it.

How do I set it up?

Sign in to the PC as a parent/administrator, download the Zaksha installer from your dashboard and run it — it installs for the whole machine in a minute, then connects on its own. Make sure your child's own Windows account is Standard, not Administrator, so the protection stays put.

What exactly can Zaksha see?

Website names only — that a device asked for youtube.com, never your messages, files, passwords or anything on a page. History is kept for a rolling 7 days, then genuinely deleted. Everything is processed and stored on our servers in India and never leaves them. No ads, and nothing is sold.
