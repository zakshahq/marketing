FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which devices does Zaksha cover?

Windows, iPhone, iPad, Android and Mac, all from one dashboard — so the same rules apply across every device your child uses, not just the PC.

Can my child turn it off? Is Zaksha tamper-proof?

Straight answer: filtering is a strong layer, not a magic wall. On Windows, Zaksha installs machine-wide and runs as a system service, so with your child on a Standard (non-administrator) account it's hard to switch off without the admin password — and if it ever stops you get a WhatsApp alert. Someone with the administrator password — or who wipes and reinstalls Windows — can still change it: the admin password is the real key, the same way the Apple ID password is on iPhone. If your child's own account is an administrator, you're alerted the moment filtering stops rather than it being held in place.

How do I set it up?

Sign in to the PC as a parent/administrator, download the Zaksha installer from your dashboard and run it — it installs for the whole machine in a minute, then connects on its own. Make sure your child's own Windows account is Standard, not Administrator, so the protection stays put.

Does it slow the PC down?

No. Zaksha filters at the network (DNS) level — it doesn't scan files or sit between you and every page. It runs quietly in the background with a negligible footprint, and there's no app window to keep open.

Can I protect more than one PC?

Yes. The Home plan covers up to 5 devices across the household — any mix of PCs, phones and tablets — and you can add more on request. Each one shows up in the same dashboard.

What exactly can Zaksha see?

Website names only — that a device asked for youtube.com, never your messages, files, passwords or anything on a page. History is kept for a rolling 7 days, then genuinely deleted. Everything is processed and stored on our servers in India and never leaves them. No ads, and nothing is sold.

How much does it cost?

The Home plan is ₹199/month, or ₹1,788 billed annually (₹149/month), for up to 5 devices across the household. Pay by UPI or card, cancel anytime.
