FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is this different from a free DNS filter?

Category blocking is a commodity — every free filter does it, and so do we. Zaksha's value is the layer on top: easy time-based schedules, per-child rules, multi-device management from one dashboard, and a managed service we run for you.

Do I need to install an app on every device?

Android phones and iPhones use the Zaksha app, which keeps filtering on wherever the device goes — Wi-Fi or mobile data. Macs use a small configuration profile and Windows PCs a small agent. You get guided, device-specific steps for each platform on the Get the app page.

Can my child turn it off? Is Zaksha tamper-proof?

On a family iPhone, set a Screen Time passcode only you know and Zaksha can't be switched off or deleted — turning off filtering just locks your child's apps until it's back on. On Android, Zaksha's setup locks it on so it can't be uninstalled. The one thing outside our control is your Apple ID password (Apple lets it reset the Screen Time passcode), so keep it private. For organisations, our supervised (MDM) setup is fully tamper-proof: enforcement can't be removed at all.

What exactly can Zaksha see?

Website names only — that a device asked for youtube.com, never your messages, photos, passwords or anything on a page. History is kept for a rolling 7 days, then genuinely deleted. Everything is processed and stored on our servers in India and never leaves them. No ads, and nothing is sold.

Is the dashboard real-time?

Not quite — stats are computed in periodic batches, roughly every half hour. You see what was blocked today, the last 24 hours and the last 7 days, rather than a live feed.

How do I pay?

UPI, or a debit or credit card — whatever you prefer. Pay monthly, or annually at a discount, and cancel anytime.

We're an office or a school — is Zaksha for us?

Yes — that's the Enterprise plan. Group policies, bulk enrolment via MDM, per-user reporting, and a dedicated isolated instance with 30- or 90-day retention when you need it. Talk to us and we'll size it to you.
