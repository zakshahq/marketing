Features

Everything you need to draw the line.

Adult content off, everywhere

Porn, gambling, social media, gaming, streaming — whole categories off with one switch, set per child. Add your own sites to the block list.

Blocks AI chatbots too

One switch turns off ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and the rest — handy in exam season — without breaking Google or schoolwork sites.

An app on the device itself

A native app on iPhone and Android, a small agent on Windows, a profile on Mac. Filtering lives on the device, so it follows your child onto mobile data and other Wi-Fi.

Schedules, not just blocks

Internet off at bedtime, on after homework. Time windows per day, set per child or per device — the feature free filters don't have.

Told the moment it stops

If a device stops filtering — the app removed, the setting switched off — you get a WhatsApp message and a push alert. No silent gaps.

Private by design

We see website names only — never messages, photos or what's on a page. History keeps 7 days, then it's genuinely gone. Run on our servers in India; nothing is sold.

Hard to bypass

Built so a curious kid can't get around it.

Can't switch it off

Turn Zaksha's filtering off in Settings and the watched apps lock. Calls and the basics still work; the apps they'd reach for won't open until filtering is back on.

Can't delete the app

Zaksha is pinned on with Screen Time. Removing it, or wiping its settings, needs the passcode only you have.

Can't quietly slip past

You pick the app types to watch. If one is opened while filtering is off, Zaksha locks everything again.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is this different from a free DNS filter?

A free filter blocks categories on one network — usually your home router — and that's where it ends. Zaksha puts an app on the device itself, so the same rules follow your child onto mobile data and any Wi-Fi. On top of that: time schedules, per-child rules, one dashboard for every device, a WhatsApp alert if filtering stops, and a setup that's hard for a child to switch off. That layer is what you're paying for. Still weighing options? Our guides cover blocking adult content on Android and iPhone, plus an honest comparison of the main parental-control apps.

Which categories can I block?

Adult content, gambling, social media, gaming, streaming — and AI chatbots like ChatGPT and Gemini, which parents switch off in exam season. Each category is one switch, set separately for each child's device, and you can add specific sites of your own to the block list.

Do I need to install an app on every device?

Android phones and iPhones use the Zaksha app, which keeps filtering on wherever the device goes — Wi-Fi or mobile data. Macs use a small configuration profile and Windows PCs a small agent. You get guided, device-specific steps for each platform on the Get the app page.

Can my child turn it off? Is Zaksha tamper-proof?

On a family iPhone, set a Screen Time passcode only you know and Zaksha can't be switched off or deleted — turning off filtering just locks your child's apps until it's back on. The one thing outside our control there is your Apple ID password (Apple lets it reset the Screen Time passcode), so keep it private. On Android you choose the level: the standard install is tamper-evident — you're alerted the moment it's removed — and the fresh-phone setup is tamper-proof: Zaksha can't be uninstalled from the phone. For organisations, our supervised (MDM) setup is tamper-proof across the fleet.

What exactly can Zaksha see?

Website names only — that a device asked for youtube.com, never your messages, photos, passwords or anything on a page. History is kept for a rolling 7 days, then genuinely deleted. Everything is processed and stored on our servers in India and never leaves them. No ads, and nothing is sold.

Is the dashboard real-time?

Not quite — stats are computed in periodic batches, roughly every half hour. You see what was blocked today, the last 24 hours and the last 7 days, rather than a live feed.

How do I pay?

UPI, or a debit or credit card — whatever you prefer. Pay monthly, or annually at a discount, and cancel anytime.

We're an office or a school — is Zaksha for us?

Yes — that's the Enterprise plan. Group policies, bulk enrolment via MDM, per-user reporting, and a dedicated isolated instance with 30- or 90-day retention when you need it. Talk to us and we'll size it to you.
