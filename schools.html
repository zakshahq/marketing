In practice

Moments every school knows.

Exam week

Distractions off in the lab for the morning — one schedule flip, back to normal by lunch. No walking row to row checking screens.

Media period

Class 9 needs YouTube for one period? Allow it for that group — gaming and social media stay blocked, and every other class is untouched.

AI-free assessments

Block ChatGPT and other AI tools in the lab during assignments and exams — it's a rule on the network, not a teacher checking screens manually.

Computer class

Coding and reference sites open; torrents, games and chat closed. The lab's bandwidth goes to the lesson, not to downloads.

Something specific to your school? Rules, categories and monitoring can be designed for you — custom reports on the traffic you care about, on your own instance.

Enforcement

Filtering students can't switch off.

A filter only works if it stays on. On the platforms schools run, Zaksha locks it on.

Tamper-proof

Windows — labs & library PCs

The Zaksha agent runs as a system service on school-managed PCs. Students work from standard accounts — without admin rights, the agent can't be stopped, removed or reconfigured.

Rollout: MSI installer, deployed across your fleet in one pass.
Tamper-proof

Android — tablets & phones

School devices are set up with Zaksha as the device owner: the phone installs it during setup and locks filtering on. It can't be uninstalled or switched off from the device — a factory reset just re-enrols it.

Rollout: scan one QR during device setup — no per-device fiddling.
Tamper-evident

iPhone & iPad

The Zaksha app filters on any network and alerts you the moment filtering is quietly switched off — you'll always know. For iPad fleets that need it locked on outright, MDM-based tamper-proofing is a genuine conversation.

Have an Apple fleet? Tell us about it — we'll scope it with you.

Straight talk

What the school sees — and what it doesn't.

What your school sees
  • The website names school devices asked for — and what was blocked, per device and group
  • Reports covering school-owned, managed devices only
  • 30 or 90 days of history, kept on your own instance, then deleted
What it never sees
  • Page content, messages or keystrokes — Zaksha reads domain names only
  • Anything on personal, unenrolled devices
  • Nothing is sold or shared, and your data never leaves India

It exists to protect students and staff alike — and it's worth saying so openly. We'll help you word the policy you share with students and parents.
