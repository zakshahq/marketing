For CBSE schools

Filtering CBSE asks for.

CBSE's 2017 guidelines on safe internet use ask affiliated schools to install filtering on their computers and review what is blocked regularly. Zaksha can help: it filters school devices set up with it, and you can review blocked sites per device from one console.

Source: CBSE circular on safe and effective use of the internet, 18 August 2017 (PDF).

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are adult sites blocked all the time, or only in school hours?

All the time, once it's on. You choose which categories follow a schedule, such as social media, games, streaming and AI tools.

Can we turn AI tools off only during a test?

Yes. Put the lab in a group with a schedule; AI tools are paused inside the window and back after it.

Does it read what students type?

No. It sees which sites a device looks up and whether they were blocked.

What if a student changes the DNS setting?

On school laptops and PCs, changing it requires admin rights. Encrypted DNS services are blocked. On Android, a device that stops reporting is flagged "Gone quiet".

We already have a firewall.

A firewall covers your network. Zaksha covers each device on any network, and keeps a record per device.

Where is our data?

In India, on AWS Mumbai. A dedicated instance can be quoted for your school.

Does it work off campus?

Yes. Rules apply on the device, so they hold at home and on mobile data.
