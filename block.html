FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can my child just turn it off?

We'd rather be straight about this: filtering is a strong layer, not a magic wall. On a device where a determined teen can change settings or install a VPN, DNS filtering is bypassable — protect settings with a Screen Time or device PIN. On Android, the tamper-proof setup locks Zaksha on so it can't be uninstalled from the phone, and on iPhone the app alerts you if filtering is quietly switched off.

What exactly can Zaksha see?

Website names only — that a device asked for youtube.com, never your messages, photos, passwords or anything on a page. History is kept for a rolling 7 days, then genuinely deleted. Everything is processed and stored on our servers in India and never leaves them. No ads, and nothing is sold.
