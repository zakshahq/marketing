The questions you get asked

Audit: "Is the policy actually enforced?"

Every device carries it, and the console shows which rule applies to each one right now.

HR: "Are we monitoring staff?"

No. Zaksha sees which sites a device looks up, not what's on them, and gives you a notice for staff.

Legal: "Where is the data?"

In India, on AWS Mumbai, or on an instance of its own.

Branch head: "Why is this site blocked here?"

Open the device and see the policy that decided it.

Why organisations choose it

Branches drift

A local manager changes the router, someone allows a site "for now", a laptop goes home. A policy that lives on each device doesn't drift with the network.

One policy for the organisation, local rules per office

Set the rules once for everyone, add stricter ones for a site or a group, and see the result on each device. A device belongs to one group at a time.

Your data, your choice

  • Shared hosting in India by default, on AWS Mumbai.
  • A dedicated instance for your organisation, for records on infrastructure no one else uses.
  • On-premise: ask us.
  • Only people you add can see activity, and you set each person's role (Owner, Admin, Viewer). Zaksha support can sign in to help, and every support sign-in is recorded.
  • Records are kept 30 days by default; your contract can set longer, up to a year.

How it works

It stays on, and you hear when it doesn't

Zaksha runs on each device and checks every website name before the device reaches it, on the office network, at home or on mobile data.

  • On Windows, staff without admin rights can't stop or remove it.
  • Encrypted DNS services and iCloud Private Relay are blocked as ways around it.
  • A device that goes quiet for two days while others in its office are active is flagged in the console.
  • On supervised Apple devices the profile can't be removed.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

We already have a firewall.

It covers the office network. Zaksha covers each device on any network and keeps a record per device; keep both.

HR will say this is monitoring.

It isn't. Zaksha records website names, never page content, messages, keystrokes or screens, and gives you a notice for staff.

Do you support single sign-on?

Google sign-in today. Tell us your identity provider (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace) and we'll set it up with you during the pilot.

Can Zaksha staff see our data?

Only to help when you ask, and every support sign-in is recorded. Nobody else outside your organisation can.

Where is our data, and can it be somewhere of its own?

In India, on AWS Mumbai. A dedicated instance is quoted on top; on-premise, ask us.

How long are records kept?

30 days by default, up to a year by contract.
