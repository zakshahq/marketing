FAQ

Questions, answered.

We already have a firewall.

It covers the office network. Zaksha covers each device on any network and keeps a record per device; keep both.

Does Zaksha monitor staff?

No. It only sees which sites a device looks up. It never reads messages, takes screenshots or records keystrokes.

Only to help when you ask. Nobody else outside your organisation can. See how we handle your data.
