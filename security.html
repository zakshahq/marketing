Security and data handling

What Zaksha records about your devices, and who can see it

Zaksha filters the devices you enrol by checking each website name a device looks up. This page sets out what that means for your data, in plain terms, for your security review.

01

What we record

  • For each device you enrol: the website names it looks up, whether each was allowed or blocked, and the minute it happened.
  • The device details you enter, such as its name and platform.
  • The name and email of each person you add to the console.
LAB2-PC14Tue 11:42chat.openai.comBlocked

That line is the whole record. We never see the page itself, what was typed on it or what was read.

02

Where it is kept

  • In India, on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Mumbai. Device activity is not stored outside India.
  • The database is encrypted on disk and cannot be reached from the internet.
  • Connections are encrypted: the console uses HTTPS, and devices send their lookups over encrypted DNS (DNS over TLS or HTTPS).
03

How long we keep it

  • Device activity is kept for 30 days by default, then deleted automatically. We clear out expired records every hour.
  • Your contract can set a longer period, up to a year.
  • When you remove a device from the console, its activity is deleted with it.
  • Backups are kept for 7 days, in the same Mumbai region.
04

Who can see it

  • Only people your organisation adds to the console can see activity, and you choose each person's role.
  • No other customer. Every request is checked against your organisation's account, so one organisation cannot see another's devices.
  • Zaksha's support team, from our admin console. When a support person signs in to your console, we record who it was, when, and for how long. A support sign-in lasts at most 8 hours unless extended, and never beyond 24.
05

What Zaksha does not do

  • No screenshots, and no recording of keystrokes.
  • No reading of page content, messages or chats.
  • We do not sell your data, or anyone's.
  • No advertising or profiling from children's data.
06

Deleting your data

  • On request, or when your contract ends, email support@zaksha.com. We delete your account with its devices, settings, activity records and the console logins that belong only to your organisation.
  • Any leftover activity is removed within 24 hours, and the last copies in backups expire within 7 days.
07

Your organisation's part

  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, your organisation is the data fiduciary: it decides how its devices are used and gets any consent that is needed, such as from parents or guardians for a school.
  • Zaksha processes the data only on your organisation's behalf, under a data processing agreement.
  • Tell the people who use your devices that they are filtered and that site lookups are recorded.

Questions, data requests or complaints: support@zaksha.com (Grievance Officer). The full policy is our Privacy Policy.
